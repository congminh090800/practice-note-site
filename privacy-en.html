Privacy Policy

Practice Note · effective 22 November 2026, updated for version 1.1.3 · Tiếng Việt

Your notes and everything you make in Practice Note stay on your device. The app has no accounts, no ads, and does not track you across other apps or websites. Since version 1.1.1 the app sends some anonymous usage data so I know what to fix and improve; you can turn this off at any time (see below).

Your data

Notes, handwriting, decks, paper backgrounds, imported documents and settings are stored on your own device. I, the maker of the app, cannot see them. A backup file is made only when you ask for one, and is saved where you choose.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera: to photograph a sheet of paper to use as a note's background. The photo stays on your device.
  • Photos: when you pick a picture as paper or insert one on a page, the app receives only the picture you chose. When you save the QR code in "Support the maker", the app only adds that picture to your Photos library and reads none of your photos.
  • Local network: used only by the AI assistant feature described below.

AI assistant (optional, off by default)

If you turn this on, the app opens a port on your local network so an AI assistant running on your own computer can read and draft notes at your request. Connecting needs a key shown only in the app. Data goes straight from your device to your computer, never through a server of mine. What the assistant then does with it is governed by the policy of the assistant provider you choose. When the feature is off, the app opens no connection on the local network.

iCloud sync (optional, off by default)

If you turn on "Sync with iCloud", your notes, decks, labels, favorite pens and pictures are stored in your own iCloud (Apple's CloudKit service), so they appear on your other devices signed in with the same Apple Account. This data lives in your iCloud account and counts toward your iCloud storage; I cannot see it. Turning sync off stops that device from syncing; what is already in iCloud stays there until you delete it in your iCloud settings.

Update check (optional, on by default)

Each time you open or return to the app and the device is online, the app asks Apple's App Store whether a new version is out, to let you know. The request contains only the app's identifier, nothing about you or your data. You can turn this off in the app's Settings ("Tell me about new versions").

Anonymous usage data (optional, on by default)

Since version 1.1.1 the app sends some anonymous information about how it is used to PostHog, the analytics service I use, with servers in the United States. The purpose is to learn which features are used and where the app runs into errors, so I can fix bugs and make it better.

  • What is sent: events such as opening the app, making a note, worksheet or deck, reviewing cards, which screen was opened and which tool was picked; the script and template of a worksheet; error reports and reports of unexpected quits; the app version, iOS version and device type.
  • What is never sent: your handwriting, text, note names, card contents, pictures, imported documents or profile names. The app never records the screen.
  • Anonymous: each install has a random identifier used to count users, not tied to your name, email or Apple Account. IP addresses are not stored; PostHog uses them only to estimate country and city.
  • Not for advertising: this data is not sold, not shared with advertisers and not used to track you.

You can turn this off in the app's Settings ("Share anonymous usage data"). Once off, the app sends nothing more. With this, the update check, iCloud sync and the AI assistant all off, the app opens no network connection at all.

Supporting the maker

In Vietnam, "Support the maker" shows the QR code of my bank account. If you want to send a thank-you, you make the transfer in your own banking app; Practice Note handles no payment, sees none of your account details and keeps no record of it. It unlocks no features.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. The anonymous usage data described above does not identify who uses the app.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with its effective date. Please send privacy questions to congminh090800@gmail.com.

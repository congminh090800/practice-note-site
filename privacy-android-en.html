Privacy Policy (Android)

Practice Note for Android · effective from the release of version 1.0 on Google Play · Tiếng Việt · iOS version

Your notes and everything you make in Practice Note stay on your device. The app has no accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases, and does not track you across other apps or websites. It sends some anonymous usage data so I know what to fix and improve; you can turn this off at any time (see below).

Your data

Notes, strokes, decks, paper photos, imported documents and settings are stored on your own device. I, the maker of the app, cannot see them. The app does not sync to any server. A backup file is made only when you ask for one and is saved where you choose; it opens in both the Android and the iOS version of Practice Note.

Photos, camera and documents

  • Photos: the app uses Android's photo picker, so it receives only the photos you pick and does not ask for access to your photo library.
  • Camera: when you photograph a sheet of paper as a background, the app asks your device's camera app to take it and receives only that photo. Practice Note does not ask for camera permission.
  • Documents: when you import a PDF or Word file, the app reads only the file you pick in Android's file picker.

Update check (optional, on by default)

When your device is online, the app asks the Google Play app on your device whether a newer version exists, so it can tell you. This goes through Google Play and carries nothing about your data. At the same time, the app reads a small settings file from the app's website (GitHub Pages) to decide whether to suggest an update; this request sends nothing about you or your device. Turn it off in the app's Settings ("Báo khi có bản mới" / "Tell me about new versions").

Rating on Google Play

Now and then the app may show Google Play's own rating card. Whether you rate or dismiss it is between you and Google Play; the app is not told what you chose. After you tap "Rate on Google Play" in the app's Settings, the app does not ask again.

Anonymous usage data (optional, on by default)

The app sends some anonymous information about how it is used to PostHog, the analytics service I use, with servers in the United States. The purpose is to learn which features are used and where the app has problems, so I can fix bugs and improve it.

  • What is sent: actions such as opening the app, creating a note, worksheet or deck, reviewing cards, which screen is opened and which tool is chosen; the script and template of a worksheet; error reports, error logs and crash reports; the app version, the Android version, the device model and type (phone or tablet), and whether the app was installed from Google Play.
  • What is never sent: your strokes, text, note titles, card contents, photos, imported documents and profile names. The app does not record the screen and does not use Android's advertising ID.
  • Anonymous: each install has a random code used to count users; it is not tied to your name, email or Google account. Your IP address is not stored; PostHog uses it only to estimate the country and city.
  • Not for advertising: this data is not sold, not shared with advertisers and not used to track you.

You can turn this off in the app's Settings ("Gửi dữ liệu sử dụng ẩn danh" / "Share anonymous usage data"). Once off, the app sends nothing more. Because the data is anonymous and cannot be tied to a person, I have no way to look up and delete one person's data; turning sending off is how to stop it entirely.

Children

Practice Note is meant for people aged 13 and over. The app has no accounts, no ads, no purchases, no chat or sharing with other people, and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The anonymous usage data described above does not reveal who a user is.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with its effective date. Questions about privacy can be sent to congminh090800@gmail.com.
